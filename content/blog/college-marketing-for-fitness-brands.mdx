## How should a rec center, a class, or a pop-up be scoped?

A rec center is not the quad. Ask for the written yes that names the room, the hours, and whether the hour is a sample, a class, or a sale. A lobby yes is not a weight-floor yes.

Marshall University Campus Recreation published the [Marshall Recreation Center Operations Manual 2025-2026](https://www.marshall.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/830/2026/07/COMPLIANT_FY26-Operations-Manual.pdf). Read on 2026-11-27, it covers that one center, not every campus. Campus recreation programs come first, then student organizations registered with Student Life, then university departments, then outside groups. A reservation needs a Facility Reservation Request form at least two weeks ahead. A fitness or wellness rental must use that center's staff, and a group may not sell, or hire an outside vendor to sell, unless the center permits it. Solicitation is barred inside and directly outside, so a table needs that form. Photography and video need approval from the Coordinator of Marketing and Outreach and member permission. Personal training, coaching, fitness classes, and swim lessons need the center's approval, and approved studio instruction needs a general liability policy. Food stays on the lobby side of the Welcome Desk. Activity areas allow only drinks in a spill-proof, unbreakable container unless the center says otherwise. Rates in the manual are not copied here.

## What should change for a run club, club sports, or intramurals?

A run club is a host, not a building. A rec-center yes does not cover a Saturday start in a park, and a park yes does not cover the weight floor. Write the host, the route, and the time window on the sheet.

Club sports are not the varsity game. A club yes does not borrow the stadium or the athletics mark. A practice inside the rec center still needs that center's yes, and a shirt sale still needs sale permission. Do not price a club at $1,500 per sorority (chapter). That rate names a sorority chapter. An intramural event is not a private court rental. Ask whether you may sample, display, or only use a table that office already runs.

## What may the crew say about a bar, a supplement, or apparel?

The crew may read only the claim sheet your counsel already approved. They may not add a disease line or a result you have not cleared.

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration published [Dietary Supplement Labeling Guide: Chapter VI. Claims](https://www.fda.gov/food/dietary-supplements-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/dietary-supplement-labeling-guide-chapter-vi-claims) in April 2005. The page says the guide contains nonbinding recommendations. Read on 2026-11-27, a health claim describes reducing the risk of or preventing a disease. The example on the page is "calcium may reduce the risk of osteoporosis," and that claim needs FDA authorization before use. A structure/function claim describes maintaining body structure or function and does not require preapproval. In labeling, the statement has to be truthful and not misleading, FDA must be notified no later than 30 days after first marketing, and the disclaimer wording stays as printed: "This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease." Your counsel still owns the sheet.

The Federal Trade Commission published revised [Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-07-26/html/2023-14795.htm) on July 26, 2023, effective that day. Disclose a connection the audience would not reasonably expect when it might change the weight of the endorsement. Payment, a free or discounted product, or the chance of a prize can be that connection. The test is a significant minority of the audience. Clear and conspicuous means difficult to miss and easily understandable. An online disclosure has to be unavoidable. A profile-only line is easy to miss, and a line behind a "more" control is one people can miss. Example 9 is a college student who earns points for posts and can trade them for prizes such as concert tickets or electronics. A free bar for a post needs that disclosure. Name the sentence and where it sits. Collegiate Agency checks the live post against it.

## How should a code, a class list, or a post be counted?

Put the campus on the code when you need to tell schools apart. The QR code has to open a page you already track. Collegiate Agency will place the code you send. It will not invent a code, and it will not treat a national code as a campus result.

A scan can mean the page opened. It does not mean a bar was eaten or a unit was bought unless your system already shows that step. A class list is the facility's roster. A post counts when it shows the disclosure sentence. Ask for units handed, scans by campus, sign-ups the facility already keeps, and posts that show the sentence. Leave off any column you cannot read.

Refuse one price that mixes a staffed hour, a sorority placement, and a month of posts. Refuse a run club billed at $1,500 per sorority (chapter), a park route on a lobby permit, and a varsity mark the athletics office did not grant.
