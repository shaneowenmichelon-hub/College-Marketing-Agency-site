## What should a fashion brand require before it hires?

The hero photograph shows a performer on stage holding a BEATBOX carton at a tour stop Collegiate Agency photographed. He is the performer, and the carton is a drink. Do not send the frame as a clothing drop, and do not name him.

Send one sheet before you sign.

- Name the product and the sizes in the kit.
- Name the campuses, and whether each one is inside the published network of 21.
- Name what the crew may say when a size is gone.
- Name the code or QR your own system already reads.
- Name who approves a sale on school property, a chapter yes, and a live post.

A student does not post, and a table does not open, until that person has approved the sheet.

Table: What a fashion brand should have on file before a campus tactic runs

| Tactic | Permission or kit | What to count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sized drop | The sizes in the kit, and one code or QR per campus | Redemptions that code already shows |
| Pop-up or mobile store | The school's written yes for the footprint, and who rings the sale | Sales or scans the brand's system already shows |
| Chapter placement | A yes that names the sorority (chapter) | Product placed and posts for that chapter |
| Game-day stop | The lot or venue permission for that school | Codes redeemed at that school |
| Paid student post | The disclosure sentence where a viewer cannot miss it | Live posts that show the sentence |

A code with no campus on it cannot later tell you which school moved.

## How should a drop, a pop-up, or a mobile store be scoped?

A drop is a limited set of units. Put the sizes, the time window, and the sold-out sentence in the brief. If a size is gone, the crew says that sentence. They do not promise a restock you have not approved, and they do not offer a size the kit does not contain.

A pop-up on school property needs a written yes for the footprint: where it may stand, how long it may stay, and whether a sale is allowed or only a display. A sidewalk store needs the city permit, plus the school permit if the line uses school property. A yes for one quad is not a yes for a second campus.

## How should a paid post and a month of wear be briefed?

PR Newswire carried Pacsun's "PACSUN OPENS THE NEXT CHAPTER OF CO-CREATED WITH OFFICIAL COLLEGE TOUR" (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pacsun-opens-the-next-chapter-of-co-created-with-official-college-tour-302900778.html) on October 7, 2026. The tour started at the University of Southern California on October 6. The CEO was set to visit at least 12 campuses, including Harvard Business School on November 1, with Stanford, Duke, UC Irvine, Chapman, and San Diego State listed as planned. A kickoff photo identifies a Pacsun Ambassador Program member at USC. The release is a book conversation, not a product drop. It is not a Collegiate Agency route.

The Federal Trade Commission published revised [Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-07-26/html/2023-14795.htm) on July 26, 2023, effective that day. A payment, a free product, or a prize has to be disclosed when a significant minority of viewers would not expect it. Clear and conspicuous means difficult to miss and easily understood. On social media the disclosure should be unavoidable. A profile-only disclosure is easy to miss, and a disclosure behind a "more" tap is not unavoidable when the post is visible. The guides describe a college student who earns points for posts and can trade them for prizes such as concert tickets or electronics. Those incentives have to be disclosed.

A free jacket for a post is a free product. Put one sentence in the brief and say where it sits. Collegiate Agency checks the live post against that sentence. The month of posts stays separate from the staffed hour.

## How should a code or a QR scan be counted?

Put the campus on the code when you need to tell schools apart. The QR code has to open a page you already track. Collegiate Agency will place the code you send. It will not invent a code, and it will not treat a national code as a campus result.

A scan can mean the page opened. It does not mean a unit sold, unless your system already shows that step. Ask for redemptions by campus and for posts that show the disclosure. Leave off any column you cannot read. A photo of a student in the product is not that count.

## What should a fashion brand refuse in a campus proposal?

Refuse one national price, a reach number with no campus on it, and a redemption forecast copied from another company. Refuse a size the kit does not contain, a sale on school property with no written yes, and a lot that borrows a store permit. Refuse a club billed at $1,500 per sorority (chapter) when it is not a chapter, a code your system cannot read, and a disclosure that sits only on a profile.
