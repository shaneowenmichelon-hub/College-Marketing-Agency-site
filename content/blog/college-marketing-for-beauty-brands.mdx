College marketing for beauty brands is the hire a skincare or makeup company makes when the campus partner must show a shade rule, a clean way to sample, and a post that discloses a gift or a fee. Ask for the shades in the kit, the sentences a student may say about ingredients, single-use applicators for anything that touches skin, and a code or QR your team can already read. Collegiate Agency prices that work as a campus event, as Greek-life product placement, or as a month of posts, and it does not publish a trial forecast.

The contract for a sorority or other Greek organization is the [Greek life marketing guide](/insights/greek-life-marketing-agency). The contract for a student post is the [college creator marketing agency guide](/insights/college-creator-marketing-agency). This page adds the beauty rules those guides do not cover.

Those three lines are on the [pricing page](/pricing). A proposal that offers campus beauty as one number has hidden a line.

## What should a beauty brand require before it hires?

The National Student Clearinghouse Research Center published [Final Spring Enrollment Trends](https://nscresearchcenter.org/final-spring-enrollment-trends/) on June 4, 2026. Spring 2026 postsecondary enrollment totaled 18.6 million students, up 1.0 percent from spring 2025. That number is national enrollment. It is not your sample count.

BeautyMatter published ["Back-to-School Beauty: The Brands on Campus"](https://beautymatter.com/articles/back-to-school-beauty-the-brands-on-campus) on September 10, 2026. Alice Manners describes beauty brands using sorority recruitment and campus organizations. Use the Greek-life guide for the organization contract, and use this page for the beauty rules.

flytedesk published ["The top college marketing agencies and platforms of 2026"](https://www.flytedesk.com/insights/top-college-engagement-marketing-agencies) on November 12, 2026. The Her Campus entry lists beauty among content campaigns aimed at college women. The roundup does not name a partner by shade protocol, single-use applicators, or a skin-type step.

## What has to be true at the shade table and the sample?

A shade table is not a sampling hour with a mirror added. The Campus Agency's [NARS Cosmetics marketing case study](https://thecampusagency.com/nars-cosmetics-marketing-case-study/), published January 3, 2025, describes campus tabling for free samples, product recommendations, or shade matching, and tutorial events with groups such as a sorority. Collegiate Agency will not treat that case study's results as yours.

List the shades you will ship. If a student's shade is not in the kit, say so. Do not call the nearest shade a match.

Anything that touches skin needs a single-use applicator and a written sanitation step. A wand that returns to a shared jar is not a sample. A sealed sachet is a handout, not a makeover. If the state licenses the service, name the state and the license, or do not call the hour a treatment.

## What should a K-beauty brand require on a US campus?

A brand entering US campuses from Korea needs the same rows, plus three that are easy to skip: ingredient names a student can say, a skin-type step that can end in more than one result, and a code that opens a page your team already tracks.

Guru Today published [Anua's College Glow Up Tour](https://guru.today/anua-college-glow-up-tour-ulta-genz-usa-2025/) on October 10, 2025. Jin Lee reports that the tour ran from September 16 to September 26 with Ulta Beauty and Her Campus at six universities in the US Southeast, including the University of Miami, Georgia Tech, and Spelman College. Students received full-sized kits of Anua's Heartleaf Double Cleansing Duo, the Heartleaf Cleansing Oil and the Cleansing Foam, as an introduction to gentle, ingredient-focused formulas. That tour is Anua's, not a Collegiate Agency result.

Pulse by Maeil Business published ["Gowoonseang Cosmetics spreads K-beauty culture across U.S. campuses"](https://pulse.mk.co.kr/news/english/11439983) on October 13, 2025. The article says Gowoonsesang Cosmetics, owner of Dr. G, had been running campus booths at 12 US universities since late August and, as of that date, planned to reach 20 by the end of October. The feature it describes as most popular is a Skin Type Analysis Zone: a quiz, then a sample for dry skin or for oily and combination skin. The company noted that American consumers of different skin tones showed greater interest in a sunscreen that leaves no white cast than in a tone-up sunscreen. Leave the article's attendance figure out of your plan.

Require ingredient lines in the words your counsel approved, a diagnostic that can say the product is not a match, and a code that opens the retailer page you chose, such as a store locator or a product page at Ulta or Sephora. If the page is your homepage with no door on it, you bought a visit, not a retail handoff. Collegiate Agency will not invent a door you did not name.

## How should a paid student beauty post disclose the relationship?

The Federal Trade Commission adopted revised [Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-07-26/html/2023-14795.htm) on July 26, 2023. A payment, a free or discounted product, or a prize for posting is a material connection. When viewers would not expect that connection, it has to be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. The guides define that as difficult to miss and easy to understand. In social media, the disclosure should be unavoidable. A note that sits only on a profile page is easy to miss. The guides also describe a college student who earns points for posts and can trade those points for prizes: disclose the incentives, and check that the disclosure is on the post.

Put one sentence in the brief, in the language of the post, and say where it has to sit. Collegiate Agency checks the live post against that sentence. A gift and a paid month are both connections. Your counsel owns the wording. Collegiate Agency will not invent a skin result you did not approve in writing.

Use the same code as the table, or a second code you can tell apart. A shared code with no campus on it cannot tell you which school moved. Name the page the QR code opens.

## What should a beauty proposal refuse?

Refuse one national price. Refuse a reach number based on the 18.6 million students enrolled in spring 2026. Refuse a trial rate copied from another brand's tour, a makeover with no license named, and a beauty club billed at $1,500 per sorority (chapter) when it is not a sorority (chapter).
