# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college marketing for beauty brands

## Hypothesis

Beauty and skincare brand marketers search this when they want a campus partner and find vendor vertical pages for sampling, makeovers, sorority partnerships, and creator posts. Roundups that answer "best college marketing agency" name content publishers for college women and do not set field rules for shade matching, single-use applicators, or a skin-type step. Collegiate Agency has no page on the hire. This page should win the click by saying which published rate buys a staffed hour, which rate buys Greek-life placement, which rate buys posts, and what the brief must name before anyone samples skin or posts. It links to the sampling, Greek-life, and creator guides instead of restating them.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What is college marketing for beauty brands?", "What should a skincare brand require from a campus partner?", "How should a K-beauty brand run a US campus tour?", "What should a student beauty post disclose?"
