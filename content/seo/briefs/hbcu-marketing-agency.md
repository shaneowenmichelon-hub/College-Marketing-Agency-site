# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

hbcu marketing agency

## Hypothesis

Buyers searching this query get either a general college agency or a media plan that says HBCU and never names a campus. A hiring guide can answer in the first paragraph, cite the NCES count of 99 HBCUs in 2022, and say plainly that Collegiate Agency's published 21-campus network does not currently list an HBCU. The page should send the buyer to name the schools, then to contact or build a campaign, without inventing HBCU clients or results.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What does an HBCU marketing agency do?", "How do I hire a campus team for an HBCU?", "Is a general college agency the same as an HBCU agency?", "What should an HBCU activation contract include?"
