# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college event marketing agency

## Hypothesis

Sponsorship and staffing pages already exist. Buyers searching for an event marketing agency still need a page that separates producing a date from buying a logo on someone else's event. The first paragraph can make that split, then send the reader to the sponsorship guide instead of repeating it. ADA Title II is the access citation for public campuses, framed as a question for the campus and counsel.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What does a college event marketing agency do?", "Should I sponsor a college event or hire an agency to staff it?", "What belongs in a campus event contract?"
