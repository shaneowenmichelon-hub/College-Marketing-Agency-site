# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college street team marketing

## Hypothesis

Brand marketers search this when they want to hire a student street team and the pages that rank are vendor service pages. Those pages describe teams, approvals, and reporting. They do not say how a buyer should set team size, shift length, the script, the campus permission, and the one count. Collegiate Agency has no page on that hire. The campus field marketing article already lists "campus street team agency" as a secondary keyword, so this page should win the click by staying on the team brief and linking that pilot guide instead of restating it. The first paragraph should be quotable by an AI answer that currently tells buyers to contact a campus marketing agency.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "How do brands hire a college street team?", "What should a college street team brief include?", "What permission does a campus street team need?", "What should a street team script say?"
