# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college marketing for restaurant brands

## Hypothesis

Restaurant, QSR, fast-casual, and food-delivery marketers search this when they want a campus partner and find vendor pages for sampling tours, grand openings, and delivery case studies. Those pages name agencies. They do not say which published rate buys a staffed hour, which rate buys Greek-life placement, which rate buys posts, or what the brief must show before anyone portions food. Collegiate Agency has no page on the hire. This page should win the click by answering the query in the first paragraph and by separating a dining-hall yes, a tracked coupon, a delivery zone, a nearby grand opening, and the local food permit. It links to the sampling, pop-up, beverage, tailgate, and pricing pages instead of restating them.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What is college marketing for restaurant brands?", "What should a QSR require from a campus partner?", "How do meal plans and dining dollars change a campus food sample?", "What permit does a campus food sample need?"
