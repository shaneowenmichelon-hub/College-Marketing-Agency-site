# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college marketing for beverage brands

## Hypothesis

Beverage brand marketers search this when they want a campus partner and find vendor industry pages for sampling, ambassadors, and game-day pours. Roundups that answer "best college marketing agency beverage CPG" name those vendor pages and do not set field rules for a can count, cold storage, an exclusive pour, a 21-and-older check, or a disclosure. Collegiate Agency has no page on the hire. This page should win the click by saying which published rate buys a staffed hour, which rate buys Greek-life placement, which rate buys posts, and what the brief must name before anyone hands out a can. It links to the sampling, tailgate, and sports guides instead of restating them.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What is college marketing for beverage brands?", "What should a beverage brand require from a campus partner?", "How should an energy drink or a non-alcoholic brand sample on campus?", "What should a student beverage post disclose?"
