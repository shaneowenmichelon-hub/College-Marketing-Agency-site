## Primary query

college marketing for fashion brands

## Hypothesis

A fashion or apparel brand searching this query is choosing a campus partner, not reading a trend essay. Beauty, beverage, fintech, and restaurant already have vertical hiring guides. Fashion does not. A page that tells the brand how to scope a sized drop, a pop-up or mobile store, a chapter placement, a game-day stop, a paid post, and a code or QR the brand already tracks should win the click over a generic vendor playbook. Public pages from RetailBoss, EMARKETER, Marketing Dive, and PR Newswire show apparel brands already buying campus stores, game-day tours, and college routes.
