# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

campus product sampling agency

## Hypothesis

The site already has an on-campus sampling guide and a sampling-ideas article. Buyers searching for an agency need the contract: campuses, units, crew, and the count. A hiring page can answer that in the first paragraph, link to the existing guides, and send the buyer to build a campaign without restating the format list or inventing trial results.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a CPG or beverage marketer would type: "What does a campus product sampling agency do?", "What should a college sampling contract include?", "How is a sampling agency different from a list of sampling ideas?"

## Competitor pages

Observed public vendor pages for college sampling, without search-volume tools, plus the CDC food-safety page the article cites for handling. Competitor trial numbers are left out of the article.

## CTA path

/build-a-campaign

The product-placement card uses Build a campaign as the primary action. The body also links /contact, /services/events, /services/product-placement, and /pricing.

## Expected outcome

A brand sampling lead lands on a hiring guide, opens the existing sampling guides only for format, and sends a campus list.
