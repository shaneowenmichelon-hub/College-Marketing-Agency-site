# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college marketing for fintech brands

## Hypothesis

Fintech, banking, card, budgeting, and investing marketers search this when they want a campus partner and find vendor industry pages for orientation tables, financial-literacy events, and student posts. Those pages do not separate a credit-card tangible-item rule from a budgeting app, and they do not require an approved claims sheet, a code the brand already tracks, or a sign-up that stays in the brand's app. Collegiate Agency had no page on the hire. This page should win the click by saying which published rate buys a staffed hour, which rate buys Greek-life placement, which rate buys posts, and what counsel has to clear before anyone offers a physical item for a credit application. It links to the app, back-to-school, and content-rights guides instead of restating them.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What is college marketing for fintech brands?", "What should a fintech brand require from a campus partner?", "Can a credit card brand give students a t-shirt for an application on campus?", "How should a paid student post about a banking app disclose the relationship?"
