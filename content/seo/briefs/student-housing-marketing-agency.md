# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

## Hypothesis

Pages ranking for this query sell a digital system: search, paid media, websites, and branding. Operators also publish ambassador-led lease-up programs, and the 2026 campus-agency roundups do not name a student-housing specialist for on-campus execution. A hiring guide can answer the query in the first paragraph, then send the buyer to build a campaign and to the ambassador service page without restating the ambassador cluster.
