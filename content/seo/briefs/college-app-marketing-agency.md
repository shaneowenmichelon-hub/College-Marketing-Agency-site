# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college app marketing agency

## Hypothesis

App and consumer-tech growth leads search this when they want student installs or sign-ups and find vendor pages for street teams, demo events, and referral codes, plus 2026 case studies of campus app-download programs. Roundups that answer "best college marketing agency" list apps as a buyer category and do not name a campus partner by app-growth fit. Collegiate Agency has no page on the hire. This page should win the click by saying which published rate buys posts with a link, which rate buys a staffed hour, and what the brief must name before anyone shares the app. It links to the pop-up, creator, and activation guides instead of restating them.

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What does a college app marketing agency do?", "How should a brand hire a campus partner for app installs?", "What should a student post disclose when promoting an app?"
