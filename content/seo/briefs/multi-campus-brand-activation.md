# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

multi-campus brand activation

## Hypothesis

Field-marketing and single-campus activation pages do not answer a buyer who already has a school list. A brief can say what is shared (offer, claim, count definition) and what is per campus (permission, crew, photos). The field guide stays the pilot. This page is the list. NIST's PII guide is the citation when the same form travels.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "How do I brief a multi-campus brand activation?", "Can one college activation contract cover many schools?", "What should a multi-campus recap include?"
