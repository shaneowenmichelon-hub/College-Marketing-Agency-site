# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

finals week marketing

## Hypothesis

Sampling and activation pages assume a social campus. Finals week is an exam clock. A guide can say what to skip, cite one registrar's exam-matrix rule as a public example, and offer only a quiet, permitted date. It links to the pop-up guide for weeks that are not exams.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "Should I run a campus activation during finals week?", "What marketing works during college finals?", "How do exam schedules change a campus event?"

## Competitor pages

Observed public pages, without search-volume tools.

- https://registrar.gatech.edu/registration/exams
- Seasonal sampling pages that treat December as another trial week without an exam calendar.

## CTA path
