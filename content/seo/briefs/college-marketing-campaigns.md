# Pre-article brief

## Primary query

college marketing campaigns

## Hypothesis

Brand marketers search this when they are choosing which campus format to hire, and the pages that rank are case-study roundups and vendor portfolios. Those pages name activations. They do not say how a brand picks one format, what the brand has to supply, or which published rate matches the job. Collegiate Agency has no page on that choice. This page should win the click by answering the query in the first paragraph and by separating a staffed sample, a pop-up or tour stop, a sponsored event, student-organization placement, and a month of posts. It links to the activation, sampling, and multi-campus guides instead of restating them.

## Target AI engines and prompts

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Bing Copilot. Prompts a brand marketer would type: "What college marketing campaigns can a brand run?", "How should a brand choose a campus campaign format?", "What does a brand supply before a campus campaign starts?", "How should a brand measure a college marketing campaign?"
