## Primary query

college marketing for fitness brands

## Hypothesis

A fitness brand searching this query is choosing a campus partner, not reading a trend essay. Beauty, beverage, fintech, restaurant, and fashion already have vertical hiring guides. Fitness does not. A page that tells the brand how to separate a rec-center hour, a class, a run club, club sports, intramurals, a pop-up, and a paid post, and how to keep the claim sheet and the code the brand already tracks, should win the click over a competitor case study. Public pages re-opened on 2026-11-27 show fitness brands already buying rec-center sampling, run clubs, classes, and pop-ups. This page does not restate the beverage guide or the ambassador cluster.
